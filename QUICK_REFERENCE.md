## ❓ TOP 8 QUESTIONS & QUICK ANSWERS

**Q1: Why not use AWS Access Analyzer?**
A: Access Analyzer is a useful AWS analysis service. Our academic prototype focuses on turning observed access evidence into an explainable policy draft with validation and human review.

**Q2: How accurate is policy synthesis?**
A: The MVP measures syntax validity, wildcard findings, and agreement with expected sample policies. We present accuracy as an evaluation target, not as a production guarantee.

**Q3: How do you compete with Ermetic ($100k+)?**
A: We are not trying to compete with enterprise platforms in this project. The contribution is a small, transparent workflow that is easy to inspect and test.

**Q4: What makes you different?**
A: Explainability and scope. Every generated statement comes from observed access, validation flags risky permissions, and an administrator remains in control.

**Q5: Timeline to MVP?**
A: 8-12 weeks with 3-5 person team. Phases: Foundation (2), Core Engine (2), Interface (2), Testing (2), Deploy (2+).

**Q6: Market validation?**
A: Yes. 80% breaches from misconfigurations, 71% struggle with IAM, 92% multi-cloud. Industry reports confirm demand.

**Q7: Go-to-market?**
A: Start with SMB (5-20k/yr value), expand to mid-market (10-50k/yr), then enterprise. Target 100 customers Year 1.

**Q8: Path to profitability?**
A: At $500/month average, 1,000 customers = $6M revenue. 3% market penetration (1,500 customers) achieves profitability.
